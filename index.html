PromoEV

PromoEV is a private, single-user tool. It reads promotional emails that sportsbooks send to its owner's own Gmail inboxes, calculates the expected value of each promotion, and shows its owner how to use them. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and never places bets or logs in to any third-party site.
