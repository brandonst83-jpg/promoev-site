Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Who uses PromoEV

PromoEV is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users and is not offered to the public. The only Google accounts it accesses are its owner's own accounts, with the owner's explicit consent.

What data it accesses

With read-only Gmail access (the gmail.readonly scope), PromoEV reads messages from known sportsbook and casino senders in order to find promotional offers. It cannot send, delete, or modify email.

How data is used

  • Promotion details (sportsbook, offer type, amount, terms, expiry) are extracted and stored in a private database so the owner can view and act on them.
  • Promotional text may be sent to Google's Gemini API to help extract offer details.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than showing the owner their own promotions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PromoEV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Raw email content is discarded after the promotion is extracted. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which PromoEV can no longer read the account.
